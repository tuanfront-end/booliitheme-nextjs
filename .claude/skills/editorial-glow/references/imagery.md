# imagery

The sources have no photographs; the studio's niches live on them. They join
the style as framed objects:

- **Framed.** A photograph sits in a hairline frame at the panel radius (12),
  or as a joined panel's cell, inset 6px from the frame on a small card.
- **True colour.** No duotone, no desaturation, no brand tint over it. The
  glow stays the one *ornamental* mass of colour; a photograph is content.
- **Never full bleed.** No edge-to-edge hero photograph, no photograph as a
  section's ground. A photograph may crop off the viewport the way a figure
  does, once a page, inside its frame.
- **No shadow** on the frame; a photograph floating over another gets
  `shadow-float`.
- **Screenshots** of a product (templates, apps) are photographs here: framed,
  true, a browser or device chrome drawn in hairlines when it helps.

Sourcing, crops and weights follow the studio's rules
(`soft-neutral/references/imagery.md` is a look file, so read it only for its
sourcing and crop sections if needed).

## Floor

- Every photograph is in a hairline frame or a panel cell.
- None is full bleed or a section ground.
- None is tinted.
