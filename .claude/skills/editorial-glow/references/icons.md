# icons

The studio's split, drawn finer:

- **HugeIcons stroke-rounded** (`@hugeicons/react` +
  `@hugeicons/core-free-icons`) for every glyph that names a thing, at
  `strokeWidth={1.5}`. Beside a feature title: `size-5`, `text-brand`. In
  chrome (nav, a field, a row action): `size-4` or `size-5`, ink or muted.
- **Heroicons `24/outline`** for arrows and chevrons at `size-4`; a shafted
  arrow means a destination, a chevron means move or disclose. A rating star is
  `24/solid`.

An icon in this style is a line drawing that agrees with the line art and the
hairlines: never a filled glyph in a coloured disc, never two colours.

## Floor

- Two families, split by glyph; no third.
- Feature icons in brand, everything else in ink or muted.
- No emoji in place of an icon.
